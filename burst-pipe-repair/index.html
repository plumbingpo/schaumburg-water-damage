A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Schaumburg or the surrounding area, Schaumburg Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
